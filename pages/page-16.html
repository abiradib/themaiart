Mai Aboassi

In this work, the bean pod becomes a symbol of absence, interrupted growth, and something that is no longer physically there but still remains in memory.

For me, the bean pod is no longer only an object. It becomes a way of thinking about things that are missing, unfinished, or difficult to reach again.

Through repetition, fragile materials, and layering, I am exploring how absence can still take up space, and how something that has disappeared can continue to stay with us
